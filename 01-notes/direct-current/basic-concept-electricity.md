Intro
Some materials attract and others repel, this is due to charges being positive and negative. When two objects are rubbed together they switch between the two.

A coulomb is a unit of electrical charge and it equals an excess or deficiency of 6,250,000,000,000,000,000 electrons. One electron has a charge of about 0.00000000000000000016 coulombs.

One electron is the smallest carrier of electric charge, it is the elementary charge.

Composition of the Atom
Atoms are the smallest forms of matter and make up everything, but they are composed of particles.
Protons
Neutrons
Electrons
In any atom, protons and neutrons are tightly bound, while electrons float in rings of increasing availability.
The nucleus is the center of the atom.
The number of protons identifies its elemental identity.
Neutrons are less influential on the chemical character and identity of atoms.
Electrons are active and can be exchanged/removed
Electrons and protons are attracted to one another over distance. Electrons and electrons repel each other over a distance, as do protons with protons. The strong nuclear force binds protons in the nucleus. Electrons and protons have opposing electrical charges.
Each electron has negative charge, and protons have positive charges. In equal amounts they counteract each others presence, net zero.
Neutrons have no charge.
So when electrons leave, protons interact with other charged atoms and particles.
